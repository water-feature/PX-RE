## Behavior

The six panel selection callers retain the existing B9 rule: update pending arguments without restarting a positive countdown. An immediate panel load reloads 125 eligible service ticks. Expiry applies a changed pending panel selection and also reloads 125 ticks; the following panel input therefore updates the pending target instead of causing an immediate paired load. Equal-selection expiry returns to idle. After scrolling stops, the final pending selection applies and a further quiet window returns to idle.

The main-screen bank/program number uses the requested identity while slot A is non-idle, its source marker identifies a panel request, and its pending bank/program matches the UI requested identity. Otherwise it uses the original per-layer loaded identity. The match check prevents a later MIDI request on the other layer from borrowing a pending panel source marker. User/Factory/Add-On prefixes, one-based numbers, text positions, font and colours retain their original conventions.

Names still follow the loaded patch. There is no pending-name lookup, asynchronous loading or claim that the synchronous load stall is eliminated. 125 ticks nominally span 62.50125 ms under the earlier 100 MHz PBCLK3/PR3=50000 assumptions; clock gating, IRQ/main-loop delays and real load duration remain unmeasured.

MIDI and stored-program SysEx retain their original 500-tick reset/idle-expiry policy and force/layer routing. Edit-buffer librarian loading still bypasses this browsing countdown. The display change does not write pending or patch state. QNX behavior and the complete FPGA record are unchanged; QNX/PIC metadata reports .2.3, while M retains the established three-field 2.2.2 display.

## Implementation and ownership

| Region | Change |
|---|---|
| `[9D000D84,9D000E00)` | 31 instructions / 124 bytes: exact saved-return allowlist, panel reload/source marker, and changed-selection expiry rearm |
| `9D023550`, `9D0235E4` | Eight-byte wrapper hooks with positive/immediate flags; displaced pointer addition replayed by helper |
| `9D053790` | Eight-byte changed-selection expiry hook, with saved slot offset restored in its delay slot |
| `[9D036690,9D0367B4)` | Replacement main-screen title within the existing function allocation |
| `9D0364B4` | Branch to the relocated original full-redraw bypass |

The marker occupies the upper halfword of the existing pending force word. The wrapper writes full force 0/1 for nonpanel requests, clearing it; expiry passes the original lower halfword to application. The six full saved return addresses are compared, not just their low halves. All 16 direct callers' force setup and the two GP-relative force-address constructions were checked in the private investigation. Source alias scans and external-call contracts have the limits recorded below.

No new RAM allocation, stack frame, timer or payload growth is introduced. The title saves the chosen program at SP+48 within its existing 96-byte frame, away from saved registers. The rearm-only scheduler bytes remain identical in the combined build. The helper uses the previously reviewed B6/B9 allocation, whose vector/startup configuration was compared against the pinned baseline; FF fill alone was not treated as ownership evidence.

In the verified local integration, every final write belongs to the direct builder's factory-guarded ownership registry. The direct build serializes once and verifies all changed-byte ownership, version fields, ELF metadata, checksums and the exact final package hash. All 15 existing redacted objects and their manifest are unchanged.
